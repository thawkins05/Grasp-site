Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Grasp ("Grasp," "we," "us," or "our") handles information when you use the Grasp application.

Grasp is designed to create interactive spatial learning experiences. Some features use a third-party artificial intelligence service to generate educational content based on prompts that you choose to submit.

Important: When you choose to generate a lesson, the text you submit is sent to OpenAI, a third-party artificial intelligence provider, so that the requested lesson can be generated. Grasp asks for your permission before this information is sent.

1. Information You Provide

When using Grasp, you may enter or speak a prompt describing a topic, question, or concept that you would like the app to explain.

Depending on how you use the app, this may include:

  • Text that you type into Grasp;
  • Text produced from voice input that you choose to submit;
  • The educational topic or question you request;
  • Information that you voluntarily include within a submitted prompt.

Grasp does not require you to include personal or sensitive information in your prompts. You should not include information such as passwords, financial information, health information, government identification numbers, or other sensitive personal information when requesting a lesson.

2. Use of Artificial Intelligence Services

Grasp uses OpenAI's API as a third-party artificial intelligence service to generate lesson content.

When you submit a prompt for AI generation, the submitted text is transmitted to OpenAI for the purpose of processing the request and generating the educational content displayed by Grasp.

We do not send your prompts to OpenAI until you have been informed that this transmission will occur and have provided permission within the app.

Information sent to OpenAI is used to provide the requested AI-generation functionality. According to OpenAI's current API data practices, data sent through the OpenAI API is not used to train or improve OpenAI's models unless the API customer explicitly opts in to such data sharing.

OpenAI may retain certain API data for purposes such as abuse monitoring, safety, security, legal compliance, and operation of its services. OpenAI states that abuse-monitoring logs may generally be retained for up to 30 days, subject to applicable service configuration, legal requirements, and other documented exceptions.

You can learn more about OpenAI's data practices at: OpenAI API Data Controls .

3. Permission Before Sharing Data With OpenAI

Before Grasp sends your submitted prompt to OpenAI for the first time, the app presents a disclosure explaining:

  • What information will be sent;
  • That the information will be sent to OpenAI;
  • Why the information is being sent; and
  • That your permission is required before transmission occurs.

If you do not provide permission, Grasp will not transmit your prompt to OpenAI. Features that require AI generation may therefore be unavailable until permission is provided.

4. Voice Input

If Grasp provides voice-input functionality, microphone access is used only when you choose to use that feature and subject to the permissions provided through your device.

Voice input may be converted into text for use as a lesson-generation prompt. When you choose to submit that resulting text, it may be sent to OpenAI in the same manner as a typed prompt.

Grasp does not intentionally send microphone audio to OpenAI unless a feature specifically requires that transmission and the app clearly discloses that behavior to you.

5. How We Use Information

Information processed through Grasp may be used to:

  • Generate the spatial lesson or explanation you request;
  • Display and operate the app's educational features;
  • Maintain the reliability and security of the service;
  • Diagnose technical errors or failed requests;
  • Improve the performance and functionality of Grasp, where permitted.

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Locally Stored Information

Grasp may store certain information locally on your device in order to provide app functionality, such as saved lessons, preferences, consent status, or session-related information.

Locally stored information generally remains on your device unless a feature clearly indicates that information will be transmitted elsewhere.

Deleting the Grasp app may remove locally stored app data, subject to the behavior of Apple's operating system and any storage mechanisms used by the app.

7. Information We Share

We may share information with service providers only where necessary to operate Grasp and provide requested functionality.

In particular, submitted lesson prompts are shared with:

  • OpenAI — to process prompts and generate the educational content requested by the user.

We may also disclose information when required by law, legal process, or where reasonably necessary to protect the rights, safety, security, or integrity of Grasp, our users, or others.

8. Third-Party Data Protection

We select third-party service providers that maintain data-protection practices appropriate to the services they provide. Information shared with third-party providers is subject to the applicable agreements, safeguards, and privacy practices governing those services.

OpenAI provides information regarding its API privacy, security, and data-handling practices through its documentation and privacy materials.

9. Data Retention

Grasp retains information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the app's functionality, fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, comply with applicable law, resolve disputes, or protect the security of the service.

Information processed by third-party providers may be retained according to the provider's applicable policies and the technical configuration of the service being used.

OpenAI currently states that API abuse-monitoring logs may generally be retained for up to 30 days, although different retention rules may apply depending on the API feature, configuration, legal obligations, or safety requirements.

10. Your Choices

You control whether you submit information to Grasp's AI-generation features.

You may choose not to provide permission for Grasp to send prompts to OpenAI. If you decline, Grasp will not send those prompts to OpenAI, although features that rely on AI generation may not function.

You may also avoid including personal or sensitive information in any prompt that you submit.

11. Children's Privacy

Grasp is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

Users should not submit personal or sensitive information about children through AI-generated lesson prompts.

12. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information processed through Grasp. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Grasp, our service providers, legal requirements, or our privacy practices.

When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Material changes may also be communicated through the app or other appropriate means.
